# Northfield School — Enrollment and Device Policy

**Policy reference:** NS-IT-2026-04  
**Effective:** 1 July 2026  
**Page:** 2 of 4

## 2026 enrollment

Northfield School will serve **300 students** in the 2026–27 academic year.

## Device reserve

All purchases must include a **7% spare-device allowance**.

## Comparison standard

Vendor comparisons must use **three-year total cost of ownership**. The approved maximum is **$100,000**.
